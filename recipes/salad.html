Salad

This is a simple salad recipe.

Ingredients:

  • Cucumbers: Start with four thinly sliced cucumbers.
  • Onion: A thinly sliced white onion lends flavor and texture.
  • Vinegar: A cup of white vinegar gives the cucumber salad a bright flavor.
  • Sugar: White sugar lends subtle sweetness.
  • Water: You’ll need ½ cup of water.
  • Dill: Dried dill takes the flavor up a notch.

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the cucumbers and onion.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the vinegar, sugar, water, and dill until the sugar is dissolved.
  3. Pour the dressing over the cucumber mixture and toss to coat.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to allow the flavors to meld together.
